### OCR + Parse the Receipt

"When a receipt image is captured, run Tesseract OCR on it
to get the raw text. Then parse that text to extract:

- the total amount (receipts have many numbers — find the
  one most likely to be the total; look for 'total',
  'amount due', the largest value near those words)
- the date (receipts use many formats — handle the common ones)
- the merchant name (usually near the top)
- a best-guess category from our list

Show me your approach to finding the TOTAL first — that's
the hard part, because a receipt is full of numbers and only
one is the real total."
